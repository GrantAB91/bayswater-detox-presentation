Before you book

Questions about your visit.

Is the spa private?

Yes. You book the spa for yourself and the people coming with you, with no other spa guests sharing the facilities.

How does phone access work?

Your booking details, directions and arrival instructions are kept in the guest app. Secure timed entry opens during your booked visit, with discreet support available if you need it.

What is included?

Your booking page will show the facilities, refreshments and practical details included in your visit before you choose a time.

Can I book the upstairs accommodation as well?

Yes. The accommodation is booked separately, and you can plan the spa and stay together from the same website.
