02

Website and booking

A website that makes the spa easy to understand and book.

The first screen will explain the private-spa experience, the Wirral location and the upstairs stay before asking anyone to book.

Website and booking

A completely new customer website, written and built around the real reasons people choose Bayswater Detox.

What is in place now

The current website looks weak and unfinished. It does not explain the private-spa offer quickly, and customers can end up in different or outdated booking routes.

What AGNC will do

AGNC will sharpen the positioning, write and build a completely new premium website, create clear spa and upstairs-stay pages, and connect each offer to the right availability and booking route. Search visibility, mobile speed and reporting are included.

What this changes

Bayswater Detox gains one authoritative website that can be used confidently in every advert and enquiry.

What the guest gets

Immediate understanding of what Bayswater Detox is, where it is, what they can book and what to do next.

How progress will be measured

Website visits that reach booking, spa and stay booking starts, completed bookings, enquiries and repeated questions.

The new website will present Bayswater Detox at the same standard as the experience itself.

What customers see now

  • Generic detox wording
  • Private use is not immediately clear
  • Several different booking routes
  • Outdated and unrelated pages
  • The upstairs stay is difficult to discover

What the new website gives them

  • A clear private-spa promise
  • Professional spa and stay photography
  • One spa booking route
  • One accommodation booking route
  • A simple way to plan both

Photography and video

Show the property at its best.

The website, Airbnb, adverts and social content will all use one professional media library produced around the property.

Photography and video

Property photography, films, walkthroughs, advertising edits, facility guides and exterior coverage.

What is in place now

The current photographs are inconsistent and make both the spa and upstairs stay look lower quality than they are. There is no coherent video library for the website, listings or adverts.

What AGNC will do

AGNC will produce full property, spa, accommodation and lifestyle photography; hero films; spa and stay walkthroughs; an arrival film; short adverts; social edits; facility tutorial videos; and drone footage where safe and permitted, with a ground-based alternative already planned.

What this changes

Bayswater Detox receives a complete organised library of master photographs and films, with ready-to-use versions for the website, Airbnb, Lastminute, Google, social media, email and future advertising.

What the guest gets

A clear, attractive view of the quality, layout, privacy, arrival and facilities before booking.

How progress will be measured

Complete shot coverage, gallery engagement, video views, advert response and fewer questions about the property or arrival.

Better media will make the spa easier to trust, easier to understand and far more desirable.

04

Airbnb and the upstairs stay

Sell the reason to stay, not simply the rooms.

The private spa should be visible immediately, while the accommodation information should remain clear and consistent everywhere guests see it.

Airbnb and accommodation listings

A complete rewrite, a new opening gallery, consistent information and better spa-and-stay promotion.

What is in place now

The Airbnb gallery opens with accommodation photographs and none of the first five shows the spa. The full gallery is inconsistent, the copy hides the strongest reason to book, and Airbnb and Lastminute disagree on important property details.

What AGNC will do

AGNC will rewrite the title, summary, description, amenities, rules, arrival and host copy; replace and reorder the complete gallery; put the spa into the opening five; align the property information across listing sites; set up calendar and guest messages; and promote the spa and stay to one another.

What this changes

Bayswater Detox gains a stronger accommodation sales page, fewer contradictory messages, simpler listing management and more opportunities to sell both parts of the property.

What the guest gets

A clear reason to book, consistent information and a straightforward way to plan the separately booked spa and stay together.

How progress will be measured

Listing views, saves, enquiries, completed bookings where available, repeated message themes and interest in adding the spa.

The listing will sell the reason to book, not simply show that rooms exist.

Opening image 01

Lead with the private spa.

The first photograph should immediately show the experience that makes the property different.

Guests understand the main reason to book before they scroll.

05

Guest app and private access

The complete visit in one place.

Directions, entry, facility guidance, support, departure and rebooking will no longer depend on separate messages.

Guest app and private access

A Bayswater Detox app that supports guests before, during and after their visit.

What is in place now

Guests depend on separate messages, key-code instructions and repeated explanations. Directions, access, facility guidance, support, check-out and rebooking are not kept together.

What AGNC will do

AGNC will build a Bayswater Detox guest app containing booking details, directions, arrival instructions, secure timed entry where suitable, facility videos, supported lighting and sound controls, guest support, check-out guidance, rebooking and information about the upstairs stay.

What this changes

The business spends less time repeating instructions and uses one consistent system to support every visit.

What the guest gets

One place for the whole visit, with a private arrival, clear guidance and help when needed.

How progress will be measured

App use, successful entry, facility-video plays, support contacts, pre-arrival questions, check-out issues and rebooking interest.

The app turns a collection of messages and instructions into one professional private-spa experience.

Marketing and improvement

Bring more of the right people to Bayswater Detox.

Search, adverts, social content and email will lead to the relevant spa or stay page and be improved using real enquiry and booking activity.

Search, advertising, social and email

New creative, active campaigns, guest follow-up and clear reporting throughout the programme.

What is in place now

Search, adverts, social content, email and guest follow-up are not working together, and customers are not consistently sent to the right booking page.

What AGNC will do

AGNC will create local search pages, improve Google visibility, run paid search and social campaigns, publish organic content, set up email and guest follow-up, request reviews appropriately, report results and improve the campaigns throughout the three months.

What this changes

Bayswater Detox gains an active and measurable source of demand, supported by new content and a clear monthly view of what is producing enquiries and bookings.

What the guest gets

A relevant message, a matching booking page and useful communication before and after the visit.

How progress will be measured

Search visibility, relevant website visits, advert enquiries, cost per enquiry, booking starts, attributable bookings where available, reviews and repeat interest.

Bayswater Detox gains an active source of demand instead of relying on people discovering the business by chance.

What happens in each month.

The website comes first, the media and guest experience follow, then everything goes live with active marketing.

Month one

Replace the website and fix how the business is sold.

  • Positioning and messaging
  • New website design and build
  • Spa and accommodation pages
  • Clear booking routes
  • Google search foundations
  • Photo and video planning
  • Airbnb rewrite planning

By the end of month one, the working website direction, core copy, booking route, complete production plan and Airbnb rewrite will be ready to review.

02

Month two

Create the content and guest experience.

  • Full photography and videography
  • Walkthroughs and facility films
  • Short advertising content
  • Drone or ground-based exterior filming
  • Guest app and access journey

  • Calendar and guest messaging

03

Month three

Put everything live and bring in demand.

  • Website and booking launch
  • Guest app release

  • Monitoring, reporting and improvement

By the end of month three, the website, booking routes, guest app, improved listings and marketing will be live, supported by the complete media library and first performance report.
